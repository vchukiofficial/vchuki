## Rhythm

```
Sunday PM     shoot 8–10 pieces in one session, one setup
Monday        edit, schedule the week, mark last week off
Tue–Sat       post, reply within the hour, capture leads
```

Four to five Reels a week. One afternoon of shooting covers a fortnight. If a week needs
two shoots, the week is planned wrong.

Every piece: table, phone, daylight, fabric. No model. Never claim pure linen.

## Phase 1 · Weeks 1–3 (to ~20 Aug) — establish the voice

Payments and the COD gate are being built. Content this phase is about **who you are**,
not what to buy. Do not push hard for orders into a COD-only funnel at festival RTO.

**Shoot batch A** — one session, all fabric, no product styling

- [ ] The blend confession — "This is not pure linen. Here's why that's on purpose."
- [ ] The wrinkle objection — crush, release, recover, then steam
- [ ] How to spot a fake — burn, water, light. Invite the test on your own product
- [ ] ₹799 vs ₹4,000 — macro, reveal at 20s
- [ ] The 42°C test — absorption and weave openness vs polyester
- [ ] What ₹799 actually pays for — real numbers from `products.md`
- [ ] Made in Jodhpur — the workshop, hands, the city
- [ ] Colour in real light — same swatch, four lights

Run the first three as **Trial Reels**. They go to non-followers first and cost nothing
if they flop. If send rate clears 1%, that voice is working and everything follows it.

**Also this phase**

- [ ] Review request live at day 7 on every delivered order — `/vchuki-cx`
- [ ] Size chart on every PDP — `/vchuki-sales`
- [ ] WhatsApp Business API application filed — **due ~5 Aug, cannot be recovered**

## Phase 2 · Weeks 4–8 (to ~25 Sep) — proof and reach

Reviews and creator content have four-week lead times. Both must be moving now or they
will not exist when October traffic arrives.

**Shoot batch B**

- [ ] One shirt three ways — flat-lay styling, buttoned / rolled / open
- [ ] Repeat of whatever won in Phase 1, three variations
- [ ] Behind the seams — the quality checks, documented not claimed

**Creator seeding — start week 4, not later**

- [ ] 30 DMs to Jodhpur / Jaipur / Udaipur creators, 5k–50k followers
- [ ] Target 10 accepts · ~₹620 each · usage rights in writing before shipping

**Trust milestones**

## Phase 3 · Weeks 9–11 (Oct) — the festival window

Buying starts ~15 Oct. Everything here should already be shot.

**Shoot batch C — festive, by end of September**

- [ ] Diwali without the sequins — for the man who wants to look considered, not loud
- [ ] Gifting — one shirt, wrapped. Indian men buy shirts as gifts constantly
- [ ] Family photo outfit — what actually photographs well in warm light
- [ ] Shaadi season — Nov–Dec weddings, the whole reason menswear moves
- [ ] Bhai Dooj — the gift nobody plans until two days before

**The occasion insight:** Indian menswear is bought for events, not for wardrobes. Every
festive piece should name the occasion in the first three seconds. "What to wear to a
Diwali party" outperforms "our new linen shirt" every time.

**Campaign mechanics**

- [ ] Publish the dispatch cut-off prominently from 20 Oct — "Order by 1 Nov for Diwali"
- [ ] Gifting bundle live
- [ ] Retargeting only, and only if contribution/order clears ₹200 — `/vchuki-finance`
- [ ] Daily lead replies within the hour — window is short and buyers are comparing
